FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a cab?

Choose your trip type, enter pickup, destination, date, time, phone and cab preference, then submit the booking form.

Are these routes available for one-way travel?

The route list is the Pari Cab Service city-to-city route list. Availability and final fare are confirmed by the Pari Cab Service team.

Can I request a round trip?

Yes. Select Round Trip and enter the return date. Share any additional requirements in the notes field.

How is the final fare confirmed?

The website records your request; the Pari Cab Service team confirms the exact fare and driver details before the trip.
